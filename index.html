About these sets

Questions are drawn from the ISC Chemistry Paper 1 board papers for 2018–2026, the 2025 Improvement paper, the CISCE specimen papers for 2018 and 2022–2027, and Heritage School pre-board and first-term papers. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently rather than copied across.

Check the current syllabus in Syllabus/ before working through the older papers — it decides what is still examinable, and some topics in the 2018–2020 papers have since been dropped. More sets will be added to this page.
