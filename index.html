About

Questions are drawn from the ISC Chemistry Paper 1 board papers for 2018–2026 and the 2025 Improvement paper, the CISCE specimen papers for 2018 and 2022–2027, and the Heritage School 2016–17 pre-board and 2024–25 first-term papers. Four of those board papers have no text layer and were read by OCR into OCR/. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently rather than copied across.

The chapter list above is the school's own First Term split for 2026–27, not the ISC unit order. Check the ISC 2027 syllabus in Syllabus/ for what is examinable before working through the older papers — some topics in the 2018–2020 papers have since been dropped.
