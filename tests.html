Sit a paper

Two timed papers on the d- and f-Block Elements: one from the school note sheet, one from the ISC board and specimen papers. 37 and 37 questions, 60 minutes each.

Every paper here locks once you start.
  • Answers change freely and nothing is marked until you submit.
  • Closing the tab, reloading or navigating away submits the paper with whatever has been answered.
  • Switching tab or minimising is counted: two warnings, then the third submits it automatically.
  • On submission you get the mark, a chapter breakdown, and a worked explanation on every question — right or wrong.
